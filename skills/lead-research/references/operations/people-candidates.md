# Operation: `people-candidates`

Input: intended company/brand and optional region, domain and **seller offering/buying motion**. If the buying motion is absent, ask for it before assigning champion or buyer roles. This operation returns public professional candidates, not contacts or confirmed advocates. The host may relate people to companies in its own storage; this skill never creates that relation.

Search: Exa `category: people`, `type: auto`, up to five results for the intended entity and relevant leadership functions. Corroborate current title/employer with a bounded general search if ambiguous. Category-specific people search may reject domain/date filters; do not blindly copy company/news settings. Keep at most five **distinct, fully identified** individuals with supported current role, scope and exact returned page URLs. A first name plus surname initial is not enough to publish a person record; omit or resolve it with additional public evidence rather than expanding a guessed surname. Prefer an evidenced personal LinkedIn `/in/` profile if returned; never guess a slug. A parent executive is not automatically a subsidiary's buyer. Do not collect private contact details, phone/email, personal traits or inferred reporting lines.

For `found`, `value` is an array of 1–5 `{ name, title, employer, role, profileUrl, sources, caveat }`, with role one of `champion_candidate`, `economic_buyer_candidate` or `influencer_candidate`. `profileUrl` is an exact retrieved personal LinkedIn URL or `null`; `sources` support the current name/title/employer. Every `caveat` states that actual advocacy, budget authority and willingness to buy remain unconfirmed. If evidence cannot establish current employment or intended-company scope, omit the person; when none qualifies, return `not_found`. No minimum quota or fabricated confidence.
