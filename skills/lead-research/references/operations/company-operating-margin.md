# Operation: `operating-margin`

Input: company or operating-entity name and optional public website, region and reporting period. Output only **a sourced operating margin for the identified reporting entity and fiscal period**; do not substitute gross, EBITDA, net or group margin for a subsidiary. A private company with no public operating figures should return `not_found`, not a guessed percentage.

Search: if an official company domain is known, start with Exa general search (`type: auto`, at most five results) restricted to that domain for the latest annual report, audited statements or investor release. `category: financial report` is an optional second route when the issuer/entity is clearly matched; in the pilot it returned unrelated U.S. SEC filings for European companies. Reject name-similar but unrelated issuers, and do not interpret irrelevant results as evidence that a public-company margin does not exist. If an official report is retrieved only as a snippet and the PDF/site blocks direct verification, do not return `found`: retain a host-side review-needed draft (not a new proposal status) for manual report verification instead of silently treating the calculation as confirmed. Keep the period, reporting entity, units and accounting basis explicit. Prefer an explicitly reported operating margin. If calculating from operating income divided by revenue, both numbers must be from the same reporting entity, fiscal period, accounting basis and units, with exact supporting source URLs. Calculate deterministically; do not let a model invent the arithmetic. Cite the returned report pages, not search snippets that merely mention a PDF link.

For `found`, use `value: { "percentage": 12.3, "period": "FY 2025", "entity": "Example plc", "basis": "reported" }`. Set `basis: "calculated"` only with numeric `operatingIncome` and `revenue` in matched units; the validator checks the percentage. Explain exceptions or unresolved group scope in `note`. Do not fill a blank with a market estimate. Do not include confidence unless the endpoint itself supplies a defined value.
